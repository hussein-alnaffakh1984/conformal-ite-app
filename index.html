Online coverage under temporal drift

Streaming the data in order with an injected distribution shift. Adaptive α_t tracks factual coverage to target; a fixed level drifts off.

α_t adapts on the observable factual coverage; the sensitivity level supplies the counterfactual buffer. Drift shown along encounter order (a proxy for time, not verified calendar drift).
Method: online conformal sensitivity analysis · MSM worst case solved exactly as a linear-fractional program (Charnes–Cooper → LP).
Demo data: Diabetes 130-US Hospitals (real covariates, 800-row sample). Computation runs entirely in your browser — no data leaves this page.
